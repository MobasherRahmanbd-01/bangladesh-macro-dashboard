📌 Executive Summary — Where Bangladesh Stands (Sep 2026) auto-updates on Refresh

SLOWDOWN + HIGH INFLATION + EXTERNAL SHOCK ENERGY VULNERABLE REMITTANCE BUFFER

  • Growth halved: 7.1% (2022) → 5.78% (2023) → 4.22% (2024) → 3.49% (2025) — slowest in 15y excl. 2020 Covid (3.45%). IMF est. $456.3bn GDP 2025, $2,597 per-capita.
  • Inflation stuck high: 7.7% (2022) → 9.88% (2023) → 10.47% (2024) → 8.77% (2025). Feb 2026 still 9.13%, July 9.16% (food 9.3%). Pre-2022 avg was ~5.5-6%.
  • Taka devalued: 91.7 (2022) → 106.3 (2023) → 115.6 (2024) → 121.9 BDT/USD (2025 avg) — World Bank PA.NUS.FCRF. Open market touched 123 in 2026.
  • Reserves crashed then stabilised: $46.1bn (2021) → $33.7bn (2022) → $21.4bn (2024) → $28.56bn (2025). Bangladesh Bank gross $37.58bn July 2026 (NIR $32.93bn) after buying $4.3bn.
  • Lifeline — remittances record: $21.5bn (2022) → $22.07bn (2023) → $27.5bn (2024) → $33.88bn (2025) — cushions current account (-3.81% GDP 2022 → -0.05% 2025).
  • Trade gap wide: 2025 exports $50.7bn vs imports $76.8bn. Imports spiked $96.1bn in 2022 on energy. Energy import bill ~$12bn/yr; +$10 oil = +$900m cost.
  • Debt manageable but rising: External debt 20.2% GNI (2022) → 22.25% (2024). Govt debt ~40.2% GDP (2026). LDC graduation Nov 2026.

🌍 Geopolitics & What to Expect

Trigger: US-Israel-Iran war (Feb 2026–), Hormuz closure (20% world oil), Qatar LNG force majeure, Ras Laffan -17% capacity, Brent $72 → $118 Mar → ~$70 Jul → $109 early Sep. China fuel-export ban tightens Asia diesel/jet.

SANEM worst case -3% GDP in 2y
  • Energy +40% oil / +50% LNG alone: -1.2% GDP, exports -2%
  • Freight +25% + EU/US demand -5%: -1.4% GDP, exports -3.4%
  • Combined + remittance -10%: -3% GDP, exports -6%, garments -4.5%, transport -5%
7 shocks to watch
  • Import/export costs, taka/reserves, power cuts, fertilizer (30% urea via Hormuz), food inflation, Gulf jobs (8.6m workers), investment freeze
  • On ground: 1.5km fuel queues, Eastern Refinery 2/5 units shut, universities early shut Mar 9, malls 7pm shut, 4/5 fertilizer plants shut
Base vs Downside
  • Base (if Hormuz reopens): growth 4.5-4.7% 2026, inflation eases to 7-8%, reserves rebuild on remittances
  • Downside (prolonged): growth 3-3.5%, inflation 9-10%, taka 125+, power rationing hits garments
  • Buffers: diversify energy/solar, $2bn+ external fuel financing, formal remittances, India diesel 15k t/mo
